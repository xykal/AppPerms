# AppsPerms paper-cut masters

Eight 1024 × 1024 RGBA PNG masters, created with AI for the AppsPerms owner's illustration request. Background removal was performed with rembg plus chroma-key mask refinement and edge despill. These are raster paper-cut illustrations, not editable vectors or screenshots of the app.

Palette reference: AppsPerms amber `#FFB020`, charcoal `#171A21`, and off-white `#ECEFF4`. Paper texture introduces tonal variation.

The security-report illustration is symbolic artwork. It is not a VirusTotal result, endorsement, certification, or a guarantee that an APK is safe. Keep the real scan data and verification links separate.

Only derivatives under `docs/images/illustrations/` and four small `drawable-nodpi` WebPs are used at runtime. Do not copy these full-size masters into Android resources.

Regeneration, placement, byte budgets and testing are documented in [docs/ILLUSTRATIONS.md](../../docs/ILLUSTRATIONS.md). The repository's [license](../../LICENSE) remains in place.
